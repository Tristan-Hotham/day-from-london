\section*{Data availability statement}
Data and code: Zenodo DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24105505. The interactive map is at \url{{{PAGE}}} and the repository at \url{{{REPO}}}. The repository holds the derived travel-time grids, isochrones, fitted speeds, the documented journey times with their sources and the model's predictions, and the code. The turnpike road map and the London coach services \citep{rosevear2023tp,rosevear2023coach} were obtained from openICPSR, whose project pages state no licence; they are not redistributed, and only derived results are shared. The 1851 rail lines \citep{satchell2023} are CC BY 4.0; OpenStreetMap data (via OpenFreeMap) are ODbL.
